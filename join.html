Consult for real start-ups in your first year

Associates work alongside our consultants on live client projects, building research, analysis and communication skills you can't get from a lecture. No previous consulting experience is required.

Why join

You'll work on problems founders actually have, learn from upper-years who've done co-ops at top firms, and join an alumni network of 114 people who remember what it was like to start out.

Year one

Associate

Market research, core deliverables and the final client presentation.

Year two

Director

Lead engagements and client communication, and mentor Associates.

Year three

Managing Director

Lead the team and create development opportunities for members.

How recruitment works

  1. Meet us in September

    Find us at the Club Crawl during AFM Orientation Week and at our fall info session. Dates are posted on our social media.

  2. Apply

    Submit your resume, short written answers and a short video telling us why you're interested in ACE.

  3. Interview and case

    Shortlisted applicants interview with our team and present a take-home case. We look at how you think, not what you already know about consulting.

  4. Onboarding

    New Associates are trained and placed on client teams for the term.
