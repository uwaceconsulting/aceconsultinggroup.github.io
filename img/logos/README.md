# Alumni placement logos

White, background-free versions of the company marks shown under Alumni Placements on alumni.html.

- `original/`: the unmodified source files the logos were made from.
- `*.png`: the white versions the site uses. Made from the originals: shapes turned white, light parts (such as
  the letters inside the TD square, or the lion inside the RBC shield) made transparent, margins trimmed.

"Public domain" on Commons means the artwork is too simple to be copyrighted. All the names and marks are
trademarks of their owners (the RBC lion-and-globe shield belongs to Royal Bank of Canada) and are shown only
to say where ACE alumni have worked. Remove any logo on request.

The tab icon (`../favicon-32.png`, `../favicon-192.png`, `../apple-touch-icon.png`) is the ACE mark from
`../logo-white.png` on a navy tile.
