Students consulting for start-ups since 2009

ACE began in Waterloo's School of Accounting and Finance as a way for students to put their coursework to work before their first co-op. Today it's one of the longest-running student consultancies serving the Kitchener-Waterloo start-up community.

Our history

  1. 2009

    Four second-year SAF students found ACE Financial Services, doing bookkeeping and budgeting for student associations and other University of Waterloo groups.

  2. 2013

    Renamed ACE Consulting Group. We start working with entrepreneurs and start-ups across Kitchener-Waterloo, focused on strategy, market research and operations.

  3. 2019

    Our first ten-year reunion brings a decade of alumni together with the current team.

  4. Today

    100+ clients served and 114 alumni working across consulting, product, accounting, law and start-ups.

How ACE is run

Members grow with the organization over three years, then graduate into our alumni network.

Year one

Associate

Conduct market research and growth analysis, build core deliverables and case studies, and present to clients.

Year two

Director

Lead client engagements, manage projects and client communication, and mentor Associates.

Year three

Managing Director

Advise on engagements, lead the team, support project managers and create development opportunities.
